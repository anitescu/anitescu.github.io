About

  • Research in numerical optimization and its analysis: nonlinear and stochastic programming, mathematical programs with complementarity constraints, and optimal control.
  • Uncertainty quantification and statistical computing at scale — Gaussian process methods for large spatial and temporal data, sampling, and optimal experimental design.
  • Simulation of multibody dynamics with contact and friction, including the convex complementarity formulations behind large granular flow codes.
  • Optimization for energy systems: power grid operation and planning under uncertainty, and the solver work — decomposition, GPU and exascale implementations — that makes those problems tractable.
  • Senior Editor of Optimization Methods and Software, and a long record of program committees, review panels and prize committees across SIAM, MOS and the DOE laboratories.
